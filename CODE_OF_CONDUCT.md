# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in the cell-ui community to be constructive, respectful and focused on improving the project.

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- discussing technical disagreements with evidence and patience;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles;
- giving actionable feedback about code rather than attacking people;
- keeping issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publication of private information and sustained personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions may consider context, severity and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to `urthr.products@gmail.com`. Do not include passwords, tokens or other account secrets. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces officially operated for cell-ui.
